# Queue and team coordination

The queue organizes conversations that require operational attention. Teams
define groups of people who can own and process that work.

## What it covers

- Reviewing conversations waiting for human action.
- Identifying current conversation ownership and assignment.
- Creating and maintaining operational teams.
- Coordinating transfers between operators and teams.
