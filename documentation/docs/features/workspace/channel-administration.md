# Channel administration

Channel administration configures the WhatsApp channels an organization uses
for conversations and message delivery. The same screen is available in both
the Workspace and Management applications.

## What it covers

- Listing the channels connected to the active organization.
- Creating a channel with its WhatsApp and Meta identifiers.
- Updating channel identity, webhook forwarding, and credentials.
- Managing the WhatsApp business profile and profile image.
- Registering or deregistering a phone number with its two-step PIN.
- Creating message QR codes and configuring commands and icebreakers.
- Registering the channel's webhook callback with Meta.
- Showing whether required credentials are configured without exposing them.
- Removing a channel after reviewing its related data and confirming the phone
  number.

Creating, changing, and removing channels requires an organization owner or
administrator role. Other organization members can review the channel list.
