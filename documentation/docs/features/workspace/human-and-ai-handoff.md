# Human and AI handoff

Conversation ownership identifies whether a Meta Business Agent or a human
operator is currently responsible for the interaction.

## What it covers

- Viewing the current AI or human ownership state.
- Taking control when an escalation requires a person.
- Returning control to the agent after human work is complete.
- Recording ownership changes in the conversation timeline.
