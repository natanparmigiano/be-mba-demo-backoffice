# Agent lifecycle management [INTERNAL]

:::warning Internal documentation

This page is part of the internal portion of the documentation. It includes the
Management application and its administrative and integration features. For
external sharing, this page will not be available.

:::

Agent lifecycle management brings together the controls used to prepare,
activate, restrict, and monitor a Meta Business Agent.

## What it covers

- Eligibility checks, onboarding, and rollout state.
- Audience and phone-number allowlists.
- Human-handoff behavior and prohibited phrases.
- Business information presented to the agent.
